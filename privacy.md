# Tempest Almanac Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 17, 2026**

Tempest Almanac is an independent weather application designed to display and analyze weather information from a user's own Tempest Weather System.

## Data Collection

Tempest Almanac does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information to the developer.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

## Tempest Account and Personal Access Token

To access a user's Tempest Weather System, Tempest Almanac requires a Tempest Personal Access Token.

The token is stored securely on the user's device using Apple's Keychain services. The developer of Tempest Almanac does not receive or have access to the user's token.

The token is used to communicate with WeatherFlow/Tempest services to retrieve weather information associated with the user's Tempest account.

## Weather Data

Weather observations, forecasts, and historical information are retrieved from WeatherFlow/Tempest services for use within the app.

Tempest Almanac may store weather information locally on the user's devices to provide features such as historical summaries, widgets, Apple Watch information, and background updates.

This locally stored information is not transmitted to or collected by the developer.

## Apple Watch and Widgets

Tempest Almanac may share weather information between the iPhone app, its widgets, and the user's paired Apple Watch in order to provide app functionality.

This information remains within the user's Apple devices and associated app storage and is not collected by the developer.

## Third-Party Services

Tempest Almanac communicates with WeatherFlow/Tempest services to retrieve weather data. Use of those services is subject to WeatherFlow/Tempest's own terms and privacy practices.

Tempest Almanac is an independent third-party application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WeatherFlow-Tempest.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. The current version will remain available on this page.

## Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or Tempest Almanac may be submitted through the app's support page.
